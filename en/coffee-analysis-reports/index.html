At the same time, three Jago Coffee/Kopi Gerobakan founders were selected as Endeavor Entrepreneurs, highlighting cart and e-cart models that bring coffee closer to consumers [3][4]. Global Brands: Capital, Expansion, and Capsules in Late 2026

Luckin Coffee is eyeing a Middle East debut after selling a $1bn minority stake to Mubadala and adding a Mubadala executive to its board [9][6]. In capsules, Nespresso will phase out home‑compostable paper pods to focus on aluminum capsule recycling, while Podback has added collection at 200+ Co‑op supermarkets and now works with 1,500+ retail sites and 260 local authority recycling centers in the UK [3][8]. On market moves, Drop Coffee opened its first international café in Japan, The Miners is entering its eighth European market, and Cotti Coffee is kickstarting regional expansion in the UK [1][10][11].

Coffee prices: softer dollar, Brazil signals pull both ways (early Oct 2026)

December 2026 arabica settled at $288.75/lb on 2 October, up 0.21% as a weaker US dollar and thin exchange inventories provided support [1]. The day before, the same contract fell 0.84% to $288.25/lb as the dollar index strengthened [2]. Beyond FX, the market weighed dry weather during Brazil’s flowering stage and a higher official production estimate that capped rallies [3][5].
