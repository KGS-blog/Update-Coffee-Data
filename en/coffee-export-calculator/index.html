Coffee Export Price Calculator

Build an indicative FOB estimate from a market reference, differential, quality premium, local costs, shipment volume, exchange rate, and margin. Add destination charges for a landed-cost estimate.

The complete Q.co calculator is embedded below. Only its calculator tools are shown; the Blog navigation and article page are omitted.

What the calculator includes

Estimate FOB and landed prices using the Arabica C-Market or ICO Robusta reference. Adjust grade, origin, volume, differential, premium, processing and quality control, packaging, export documents, domestic logistics, pallet costs, margin, destination port, freight, customs, fees, and insurance. The full Q.co calculator also creates a printable quotation and includes a grade-to-premium tool.

How to read the estimate

Results are planning estimates, not a commercial offer or guaranteed transaction price. Market references and exchange rates can change; confirm them along with quality, logistics, tax, and contract terms before quoting.

Starting Arabica reference: 6.479 USD/kg from Yahoo Finance KC=F, converted from cents per pound. Robusta uses the ICO daily indicator converted from USD per tonne; it is not an ICE futures contract.
