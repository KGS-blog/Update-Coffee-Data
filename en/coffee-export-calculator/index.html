How the estimate is built

FOB estimate per kg = (reference price + differential + quality premium + local costs converted to USD) × (1 + margin). Total shipment value multiplies the per-kg estimate by volume. The landed estimate adds destination costs entered by the user.

Starting Arabica reference: 6.509 USD/kg from Yahoo Finance KC=F, converted from cents per pound. Robusta uses the ICO daily indicator converted from USD per tonne; it is not an ICE futures contract.
