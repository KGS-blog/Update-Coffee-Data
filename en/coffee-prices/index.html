Indonesian Coffee Prices & Tracking

Track Arabica and Robusta listing price references for green and roasted beans by period, with source coverage and guidance on interpreting the data.

What is included

Charts compare median listing prices per kilogram for Arabica and Robusta, each split into green beans and roasted beans. Tables accompany the charts so period values and observation counts can be read directly.

Prices are indicative references compiled from multiple sources. Observation counts refer to price listings, not completed transactions. Post-harvest processes are not compared in the summary charts.

Use the tracker to compare price direction across periods. For a transaction decision, check the original source, product form, grade, pack size, location, and trading level.

View price charts and coverage

Sources and method

The dataset combines reviewed public sources and field records. Per-kilogram values follow the size and screening rules shown with the charts. Other currencies are kept separate when available.

Each period has a source list linked from the chart methodology section.
