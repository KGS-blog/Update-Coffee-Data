Before you enroll

Certificates are not interchangeable

A course completion certificate, a certificate issued under an SCA/CQI program, and a BNSP competency certificate are not interchangeable. Confirm the exact class, trainer or assessor, certificate issuer, assessment venue, fees, schedule, and current authorization directly with the provider or certifier.

Questions to ask before paying

  • Who issues the certificate for this exact class?
  • Is the trainer or assessor currently authorized for this course or scheme?
  • Does the price include materials, exam, retake, and certificate fees?
  • Where and when does the practical assessment take place?
How this directory is organized

This first directory is based on a reader-supplied report updated October 2026. Provider details and classifications have not all been independently confirmed and may change. Listings are a starting point for research, not an endorsement or guarantee of accreditation, current course availability, or certificate recognition.
